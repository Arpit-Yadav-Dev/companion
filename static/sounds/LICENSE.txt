yes-clap.wav
--------------------------------------------------------------------
	Source: Bigsoundbank.com — "Applause from 40 People #6"
	https://bigsoundbank.com/applause-from-40-people-6-s3522.html

	License: CC0 (public domain)
	Free and royalty-free: may be edited, remixed, and redistributed,
	including for commercial use, without permission or payment.
	Attribution is not required (though appreciated).

	Original clip trimmed to the first 1.5s of actual applause (the
	source has a ~1.7s silent lead-in before the clapping starts) with a
	short fade-out, to suit a quick UI feedback sound.


All files were re-balanced together so no cue is noticeably louder than
another: the applause recording sat roughly 18 dB below the Kenney
sounds, which is why it was hard to hear. One isolated transient in it
sits near full scale while the crowd sits ~30 dB below, so it is peak-
limited before being gained — raising the file as a whole would clip that
spike before the body of the sound came up at all.

entry-pop.wav   Kenney maximize_001.ogg, loudness-normalised with a short
                fade-out. Replaces a 0.13s click that was too brief to
                register as anything but a tick.
later-shrug.wav Kenney minimize_001.ogg, loudness-normalised — a gentle
                downward "okay then" rather than a buzzer.
yes-chime.wav   Kenney confirmation_001.ogg + glass_006.ogg, an alternative
                to the applause for anyone who prefers a clean chime.
                Not wired up by default.


goal-reached.wav
--------------------------------------------------------------------
	Kenney confirmation_001.ogg + confirmation_004.ogg + glass_006.ogg
	(all CC0, Interface Sounds pack, see above), layered as a rising
	three-note phrase — a bigger moment than a regular YES, played only
	when the daily water goal is reached.

	Built from chimes rather than the applause: the two played
	back-to-back (clap for the YES, then this) and two crowd recordings
	in a row read as one long noise instead of an escalation.
